HW9 — A Love Letter
Baosheng Jin

Open index.html in a browser with WebGL 2. Drag the scene or focus it and use the arrow keys. The view aligns automatically near the reveal angle. Rotate away and back to replay, or use Reset view.

In A Love Letter, I placed the letters of “I love you” at different depths in a WebGL scene. Dragging changes the viewing angle until the letters line up and the phrase becomes readable. I used the starter code’s Shape.text() with a custom italic font atlas. Once the phrase aligns, six floating hearts burst into sparks that curve behind the text and gather into a single heart-shaped firework.
